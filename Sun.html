The Sun

Our Star

-It takes about 8 minutes for the sunlight to go from the Sun to Earth

-The Sun is roughly 109x wider than the Earth

-The Sun looks yellow but in reality it looks white in space

-The distance from the Sun to Earth is about 93 million miles

-The Sun has a diameter of 865,000 miles

How the Sun Shines

Deep inside the Sun, hydrogen atoms are squeezed together to make helium. This is called nuclear fusion, and it releases huge amounts of energy as light and heat. Every second, the Sun turns about 4 million tonnes of matter into energy. It has enough fuel to keep shining for about another 5 billion years.
